For users in the European Union, we process your personal data under the following legal bases:

Processing necessary to provide our service (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR):
- Account creation and management
- Class enrollment and scheduling
- Payment processing
- Service delivery

### 3.2 Legitimate Interests
Processing necessary for our legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR):
- System security and fraud prevention
- Service improvement and analytics
- Direct marketing (with opt-out option)

### 3.3 Legal Obligation
Processing required by law (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR):
- Tax compliance
- Financial record keeping
- Response to legal requests

### 3.4 Consent
Processing based on your explicit consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR):
- Marketing communications
- Optional data collection (profile photos, medical information)
- Cookies and tracking (where required)

**You have the right to withdraw consent at any time.**

## Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

### 6.1 Technical Safeguards

- **Encryption in Transit:** HTTPS/TLS for all communications
- **Encryption at Rest:** AES-256 encryption for database backups
- **Password Security:** Bcrypt hashing with cost factor 12
- **Access Control:** Role-based permissions and authentication
- **Account Protection:** Automatic lockout after 5 failed login attempts
- **Session Management:** Secure token-based authentication
- **Regular Updates:** Security patches applied promptly

### 6.2 Organizational Safeguards

- **Access Restrictions:** Principle of least privilege
- **Employee Training:** Security awareness programs
- **Incident Response:** Formal incident response plan
- **Regular Audits:** Security assessments and penetration testing
- **Data Backups:** Encrypted automated backups every 6 hours
- **Monitoring:** Real-time security monitoring and alerts

### 6.3 Your Responsibilities

To protect your account:
- Use a strong, unique password (minimum 12 characters)
- Do not share your login credentials
- Log out when using shared computers
- Report suspicious activity immediately
- Keep your contact information up to date

## Your Rights

Depending on your location, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

### 7.1 GDPR Rights (EU Users)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation, you have the right to:

**Right to Access (Article 15)**
- Request a copy of your personal data
- Receive information about how we process your data

**Right to Rectification (Article 16)**
- Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
- Update your profile information

**Right to Erasure (Article 17) - "Right to be Forgotten"**
- Request deletion of your personal data
- Exceptions: Legal obligations, legitimate interests

**Right to Restrict Processing (Article 18)**
- Limit how we use your data
- Temporary restriction during disputes

**Right to Data Portability (Article 20)**
- Receive your data in machine-readable format
- Transfer data to another service provider

**Right to Object (Article 21)**
- Object to processing based on legitimate interests
- Object to direct marketing (absolute right)

**Right to Withdraw Consent (Article 7)**
- Withdraw consent at any time
- Does not affect past processing

**Right to Lodge a Complaint (Article 77)**
- File a complaint with a supervisory authority
- Contact your local Data Protection Authority

### 7.2 Exercising Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights:

1. **Email:** privacy@tuitionms.com
2. **In-App:** Settings > Privacy > Data Rights
3. **Written Request:** [Physical address]

**Response Time:** We will respond within 30 days (may extend to 60 days for complex requests)

## Glossary

**Personal Data:** Information relating to an identified or identifiable individual

**Processing:** Any operation performed on personal data (collection, storage, use, etc.)

**Data Controller:** Entity that determines purposes and means of processing (us)

**Data Processor:** Entity that processes data on behalf of controller (service providers)

**Data Subject:** Individual whose personal data is processed (you)

**Consent:** Freely given, specific, informed agreement to processing
